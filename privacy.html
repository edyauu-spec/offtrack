Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

The short version

Off Track keeps your trips, leave and settings on your iPhone. There is no account, no tracking and no ads, and we do not collect your data.

On your iPhone

Your leave, trips, memories, saved places and settings. Off Track’s widgets read a copy that only they can see. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes this data. Remove all data in Settings, or deleting the app, erases it. We do not hold a copy.

What leaves your iPhone

Looking up a place that is not in the built-in list sends the name you type to Wikivoyage and Wikipedia, and nothing else from the app. Like any web request, it also carries your IP address. Links open in a browser window inside the app, and those sites have their own privacy policies. Add to Calendar only adds an event if you save it, and “Find trips for” is processed on your iPhone.

Feedback

Contact us opens an email to offtrackapp@proton.me with your app version, iOS version and phone model at the bottom. For a wrong holiday or a missing region it also adds your home region and the year. You can edit it before sending. We only receive it if you send it, and use it only to reply and fix problems. It never includes your trips or leave.

Children, changes and contact

Off Track is not aimed at children. If this policy changes we will update it here and change the date above. Questions about privacy: offtrackapp@proton.me. Off Track is made by one person, so “we” means me.
