Support

Off Track helps you track annual leave, plan breaks around public holidays and find somewhere to go.

Get help or send feedback

Email offtrackapp@proton.me. In the app, Settings → Send feedback adds your app version, iOS version and phone model for you.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign in to or recover. Back up your iPhone to keep it.

My region’s holidays are missing or wrong. Add your own under Settings → Home region → My holidays, and tell us so we can fix the built-in list.

The widget is blank. Open the app once, then re-add the widget.
