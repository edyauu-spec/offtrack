Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

The short version

Off Track keeps your trips, leave and settings on your iPhone. There’s no account, no tracking and no ads, and we don’t collect your data.

On your iPhone

Your leave, trips, memories, saved places and settings. Off Track’s widgets read a copy that only they can see. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes this data. Remove all data in Settings, or deleting the app, erases it. We don’t hold a copy.

What leaves your iPhone

Looking up a place that isn’t in the built-in list sends the name you type to Wikivoyage and Wikipedia, and nothing else from the app. Like any web request, it also carries your IP address. Links open in a browser window inside the app, and those sites have their own privacy policies. Add to Calendar only adds an event if you save it, and “Find trips for” is worked out on your iPhone.

Feedback

Send feedback opens an email to offtrackapp@proton.me with your app version, iOS version and phone model at the bottom. You can edit it before sending. We only receive it if you send it, and use it only to reply and fix problems. It never includes your trips or leave.

Children, changes and contact

Off Track isn’t aimed at children. If this policy changes we’ll update it here and change the date above. Questions about privacy: offtrackapp@proton.me.
